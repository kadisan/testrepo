YOUR QUESTIONS ARE WELCOME

Let us talk about what matters.

?
Will I manage the walking?

The planned Bakony routes are 15–20 km. We clarify distances, terrain and winter conditions in advance. During the call we discuss your walking experience; this series is for people who walk regularly.

Can I come alone?

Yes. You can join on your own. Alongside the shared journey, there is room for your own quiet time. Rooms and practical arrangements are discussed in advance.

How religious is the programme?

My approach has Christian roots. All denominations and none are welcome, and neither prayer nor personal sharing is compulsory.

Do I have to attend all three weekends?

You can choose one, two or all three. Each weekend is a complete programme, while the three meetings form a continuing series.

What does the Plus package include?

Alongside all three weekends, you receive a separately arranged individual guided day and four Tai Chi sessions. We agree the pass location, dates and validity before you join. The spring discount applies to the specified programme fee.

How do booking and cancellation work?

First, we talk. Before booking, you receive the detailed programme, price breakdown and payment and cancellation terms in writing. Requesting a call is an enquiry, not a reservation or a commitment to pay.
